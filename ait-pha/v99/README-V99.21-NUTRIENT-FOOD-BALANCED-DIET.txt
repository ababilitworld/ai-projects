AIT-PHA v99.21 — Nutrient-Aware Food CRUD & Balanced Diet Planning

Food fields:
- Title/display name
- English name
- Bengali name
- Category
- Serving description
- Serving weight in grams
- Energy in kcal per serving
- Ingredients/preparation
- Protein, carbohydrate, fat, fiber and sugar
- Sodium, calcium, iron, potassium
- Vitamin C, vitamin A and cholesterol
- Nutrition notes

Planner integration:
- Profile food selection displays serving weight, calories and macros
- Generated reports calculate daily calorie and macronutrient targets
- Selected foods are distributed into meal-count-based balanced meals
- Each meal shows serving multiplier, gram weight, calories and P/C/F
- Report compares planned calories, protein, carbohydrate, fat and fiber with targets
- Existing reports remain readable; older reports show a compatibility notice

Bundled nutrient data are editable planning estimates and should be verified for recipe, brand and cooking method before clinical use.
