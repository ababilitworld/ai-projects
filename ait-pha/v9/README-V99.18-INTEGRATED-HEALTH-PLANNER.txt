AIT-PHA v99.18 — Integrated Health Progress Planner

New flow:
Tools → Integrated Health Progress Planner
  1. Body Metrics Roadmap
  2. Nutrition & Energy Planner
  3. Lifestyle Routine Planner

The planner coordinates current and target BMI, BMR, BSR and body-fat estimates with configurable food, walking, breathing and sun-bath plans.

Features:
- Full-width/full-height themed workspaces
- Weekly and monthly milestone generation
- Shared localStorage profile across all three planners
- Existing Workspace, Calculator, Exercise, Navigation, Interaction and Appearance tools preserved
- Nine AIT-PHA themes synchronized into planner workspaces
- No component-specific color palette outside shared theme tokens
