AIT-PHA v99.22 — Standard Bilingual Food Titles

Changes:
- Food titles are no longer manually entered.
- Every title is automatically formed as: English Name (বাংলা নাম).
- The title updates live while English or Bengali names are edited.
- Existing saved foods are migrated to the same format when loaded.
- Bundled Bangladeshi food names, serving descriptions, serving weights, calories,
  macros and micronutrient fields remain editable.
- Food list, profile food selection, report meals and balanced-diet output all use
  the same standardized bilingual title.
- Bundled nutrient values are planning estimates based on standard edible portions;
  recipe, oil, salt, brand and cooking method can materially change actual values.
