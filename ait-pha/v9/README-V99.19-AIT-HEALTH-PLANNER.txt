AIT-PHA v99.19 – AIT – Health Planner

New profile-based planner:
- Create, edit, copy and delete profiles
- Current and target BMI, BMR, BSR, waist, weight and body-fat fields
- Daily / weekly / monthly cycle
- Walking and breathing exercise parameters
- Sun exposure, fasting duration (12/15/18 hours), meal count and eating window
- Selectable Bangladeshi food catalogue
- Weekly and monthly milestone plan generation
- Theme-synchronized fullscreen workspace

The planner is an educational organizational tool and does not replace clinical advice.
